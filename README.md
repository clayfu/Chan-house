# 🏠 Chan House 

## Mission

The purpose of "Chan House" is to use this modern online platform to let the worldwide people with fate and wisdom understand: the Sudden Enlightenment Chan Dharma is an inconceivable “metaphysical science of the mind” that every practitioner with true Prajna wisdom must personally realize or enlighten to. Because the Great Collection Sutra states: "The Tathagata is able to awaken to such Dharma-realm; so is called a Buddha."  The Laṅkāvatāra Sūtra says: "What all Buddhas have realized, I have also realized."  And Master Fa Chan said, "In this absolute Essence-nature, spirit and matter are one, ... From the perspective of absolute Essence-nature, all in the mundane and transmundane world are merely a kind of phenomena and functions manifesting according to conditions from one Essence-nature.  Therefore, when a practitioner achieves great thorough enlightenment, the self’s body and mind simultaneously dissolves into the whole Essence, Form, and Function - that is to say, at this moment there is only Essence, Form, and Function, and without you, me, or him." But only modern wise people will believe in and likewise realize or self-realize this Dharma-realm which is the origin of the universe and life—the world behind the quantum world discovered by scientists today.

## Author’s Work

### *Chan: The Inconceivable Metaphysical Science of the Mind — The Path to become Buddha in One Lifetime* (Traditional, Simplified, English, Bilingual editions)

The most important message this book wants to convey to readers is what Master Fa Chan said: "Seeing the nature to become Buddha is the most basic, most important, and ultimate requirement of studying Buddhism." "In Chan meditation, having the thought of seeing the nature is enough." (The Path of becoming Buddha) "The purpose of our practice is nothing more than to liberate birth and death."  "Humans are destined to die at birth. What is the meaning of a person's whole life? It shouldn't just be about making a living; there should be higher aspirations... The higher aspiration is to solve the problem of birth and death."  

The key to resolving the problem of birth and death lies in one's own Prajna wisdom. That is, he or she must understand and believe that the present universe and life of which have the samsaric birth and death are merely an illusion, like a dream. This is what the Buddha said in the Maha-prajna-paramita Sutra: "Good man! The Buddha said that all dharmas are like illusions and dreams. If there are sentient beings who truly understand this, ...then they will know the Reality*** of all dharmas spoken of by all Buddhas." And it is also what the Great Torch Dharani Sutra says: "The Buddha said: 'Brahma! All sentient beings are asleep in the long night. If even one of them awakens, he will know that all sentient beings are asleep, deeply asleep. Now that I have obtained such awakening, how can I enable all sentient beings to break the sleep and obtain this awakening as well? Brahma, this is the first meaning of awakening, so is called Buddha."
  
Therefore, a person endowed with the wisdom of Prajñā—as long as they constantly harbor a thought to awaken (to see the nature)—has the opportunity in this very lifetime to awaken from the great dream of life, just as the Buddha did; they can attain what is said in the *Mahāparinirvāṇa Sūtra*: “The Buddha is called ‘the Awakened One’; having awakened himself, he is also able to awaken others. ...Because of this awakening, they are free from birth, old age, sickness, and death; therefore, they are called Buddhas.” Does this not “become Buddha in a single lifetime and liberate from birth and death”? The practice of superior Buddhism or Chan (Chan meditation) is, in fact, just that simple.

Therefore, it's not that birth and death cannot be resolved, but whether you are willing to understand, believe, and awaken?  The Sixth Patriarch said, "Your own minds are Buddhas; do not doubt." The Complete Works of Master Tai Xu states: "All sentient beings have the potential to become Buddhas; therefore, all sentient beings are future Buddhas." May you, the future Buddha, also take the first step of faith (understanding, practice, and realization)! It's just a matter of a single thought; especially for that of your wondrous cause and conditions or karmic conditions that have spanned countless lifetimes!!

***Reality: The best modern interpretation of "the Reality of all dharmas" is actually the magical and inconceivable source of the universe and life behind the quantum world—a world that is neither material (non-matter) nor spirit (non-mind), therefore without arising or disappearing and birth or death, yet capable of knowing everything, —a world that is not a world. (The Diamond Sutra: "All dharmas spoken by the Tathagata are unacceptable, unspeakable, neither dharmas nor non-dharmas") The Buddha also refers to it as the Dharma-realm, True Self, the Great Self, Buddha-nature, Original Mind, emptiness, Nirvana, True Suchness, Bodhi, or Prajnaparamita (reaching the other shore) …; Chan calls it the Original Face, and Master Fa Chan calls it the homeland of life. Therefore, becoming Buddha in one lifetime is waking from the dream of this life, returning to the original homeland of life, personally seeing one's Original Face, and entering the magical world behind the quantum world. That is what the Maharatnakuta Sutra said:"Realizing into the Dharma realm"; what the Ksitigarbha Ten Wheels Sutra said: "Becoming enlightenment into the realm of the Tathagata." And the Mahaprajnaparamita Sastra said: "The Reality of all dharmas is Buddha. Why? Obtaining the Reality of all dharmas is called obtaining Buddha." And this True Self, the original homeland of life or the other shore, is of course free from the problem of birth and death and transmigration, and is capable - as the Maharatnakuta Sutra said, "The Tathagata knows everything and sees everything," and as the Sixth Patriarch said, "The mind is vast and pervades the entire Dharma-realm; when it functions, then will be clear and distinct, and when it exerts conduct and operation, then can know all things… One then obtains freedom and Magical Communication (supernatural powers), Samadhi of play."So, do you want to reach the Other Shore?Why not give it a try? Master Fa Chan said, "Those who wish to enter the Chan gate for practice must establish the concept of direct entry in one-time transcendence. Even if they cannot see the nature in this lifetime, they will certainly plant the seed of practice and seeing the nature in the eighth consciousness, and when the time and conditions come, they will definitely see the nature."
